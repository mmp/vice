# Departure Procedures
::: if S46:1
KSEA - Jets will depart climbing to 7000 and will need to be separated from the CHINS and HAWKZ/OLM arrivals.

Prearranged Coordination - Y and A may penetrate or transit W,E,D,O,and F airspace with respect to their traffic when using quick look

A and Q gate departures can generally be climbed and sent direct right away.
::: endif S46:1
::: if S46:2
KSEA - Jets will depart climbing to 9000 and will need to be separated from the GLASR and MARNR/JAWBN arrivals.

Prearranged Coordination - Y and A may penetrate or transit W,E,D,O,and F airspace with respect to their traffic when using quick look
::: endif S46:2
::: if S46:3
KSEA - Jets will depart climbing to 9000 and will need to be separated from the GLASR and MARNR/JAWBN arrivals.

Sleepytime SIDs (JEFPO,OZWLD,ISBRG) are all Hybrid SIDs and require vector on course. 

Prearranged Coordination - Y and A may penetrate or transit W,E,D,O,and F airspace with respect to their traffic when using quick look
::: endif S46:3
